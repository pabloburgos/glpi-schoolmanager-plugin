#  Credits

## School Manager

**School Manager** is an unofficial GLPI plugin created to adapt IT service management to **schools and educational centres**.

## Authors

- **Pablo Burgos**
- **Alejandro Galán**

## Project origin

The project started as part of an educational GLPI implementation and evolved into a more **generic, configurable and reusable plugin** for school environments.

## Acknowledgements

- The **GLPI open-source ecosystem** and community.
- Teachers, technicians and school staff whose workflows inspired the plugin structure.

This repository is **not an official GLPI project** and is not endorsed or maintained by the official GLPI team.
