#  Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful and constructive collaboration.

##  Expected behaviour

- Be respectful.
- Be constructive.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Protect private school, staff and student data.
- Report issues in a helpful and professional way.

##  Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment or insults.
- Publishing private data.
- Sharing credentials, internal IPs or private documents.
- Spam or unrelated content.

Reports may be handled by the repository maintainers.
